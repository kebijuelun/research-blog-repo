\caption{\textbf{Output quality versus sequential evaluation reduction.}
LLaDA-8B-Instruct, GSM8K, 256 tokens, \(T=1.0\).
Columns: \(K\) answers per question. Rows: mean accuracy and Pass@\(K\),
normalized to DirectCTMC at the same \(K\) (absolute DirectCTMC score in the lower-left corner).
The horizontal axis is the ratio of median cumulative sequential target-model
evaluations (DirectCTMC / method).
From left to right, \(\tau\)-leaping uses 256, 192, 64 and 16 steps and
trapezoidal uses 64 and 8 steps;
at \(S+1\) and \(2S+1\) evaluations per answer, 16-step \(\tau\)-leaping and
8-step trapezoidal are cost matched.}
