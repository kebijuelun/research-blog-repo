\caption{
\textbf{Batching cost and complete-generation speedup depend on the hardware.}
(a, b) Forward-only relative cost of one three-candidate target call against one single-candidate call, versus complete sequence length for LLaDA-8B-Instruct and Dream-7B-Instruct on a B200 and an L40S GPU. The dashed line marks the cost of three separate calls and the dotted line one single-candidate call. At short lengths on the B200, one three-state call costs less than three separate calls. As sequence length grows, the three-state call approaches the cost of three separate calls on both GPUs, with the L40S reaching that regime at shorter lengths.
(c) Complete-generation speedup of SpecCTMC over DirectCTMC versus speculation depth for Dream-7B-Instruct on HumanEval and GSM8K with 256 generated tokens, on a B200 (solid) and an L40S (dashed); shaded bands are paired 95\% intervals. The same depth, acceptance, and evaluation reduction give different wall-clock outcomes on the two devices, and depth 8 on GSM8K exceeds L40S memory.}
\label{fig:forward-cost-hardware}
\label{fig:depth-hardware}
