\caption{
{\bf Stochastic sampling expands solution coverage, and exact speculation reduces its sequential cost.}
All results use LLaDA-8B-Instruct on five-shot GSM8K with 256 generated tokens. The dashed line is the strongest matched deterministic configuration, Fast-dLLM factor \(f=1.0\) (81.25\%; \cref{tab:deterministic-matched}). (a): empirical Pass@\(K\) for four selected fixed token temperatures; \(T=0.5\) is highlighted. (b): measured DirectCTMC and SpecCTMC results at \(T=1.0\), with points corresponding to \(K\in\{1,2,4,8,16,32,64\}\). At \(K=32\), both attain 85.94\% observed coverage, while median cumulative sequential evaluations decrease from 8,140 to 2,901 (\(2.81\times\)).
}
