\caption{Speedup over plain decoding vs.\ draft length $k$ on legal text, under
the two verifier regimes (shared $y$-axis). Without a KV cache (left), draft
tokens are nearly free and speedup grows with $k$; with a KV cache (right),
each draft position has real verification cost and every curve slopes downward:
profit requires high acceptance (domain-matched olifant-eurlex), and the
expensive neural draft falls far below break-even (dashed line).
$k \geq 12$ uncached points are from a preliminary \texttt{+D} sweep, speedups
computed against the same plain baseline.}
